RULES
1. Draw order: cape, body, head (HEAD_BARE for Apprentice, HEAD_HATTED otherwise), then hat/accessories.
2. Never change: head pixels (rows 14-35 cols 6-29, except hats over rows 0-18), outline silhouette of head/hands/boots, feet row 59, total height (hat tip may rise only into rows 0-13).
3. Outfits may only recolor or add layers on torso/arms/legs (rows 36-55), add accessories (belt items, pauldrons, staff/wand in hands, scarf stays), hats in rows 0-18 above/onto brim, and capes behind body within cols 2-33.
4. Keep 1px dark outline, 3-tone shading (light from upper-left), same shoulder row 38, waist 46, hem 50, boots 56-59.
5. Mirror horizontally for facing-left (swap hand roles).
